With Kp 20 the loop gain is ~5.3; FOPDT ultimate gain is ~25 %/°C (~28 min period), so Kp 20 + integral sits at ~0.8 Ku → sustained ±0.5 °C (pp ~2 °C) oscillation, output clipping to 0 for ~14% of time. Uncovered daytime fits are unreliable (ambient drift).
SIMC suggestion from the covered fit: Kp ~5–7, Ti ~25–30 min (Ki ~0.003–0.004), Kd 0 or small; tune for the covered (worst-case) plant since firmware cannot detect cover state.
