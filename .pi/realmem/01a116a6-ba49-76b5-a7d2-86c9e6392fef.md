birdburner heater SSR is SSR-25DA, control input on GPIO15 (ESP32-C6 strapping pin; mind reset/boot levels). GPIO15 five-second toggling reaches the SSR input; the 220V lamp load-switching test passes after a wiring fix (earlier lamp-not-switching was a connection issue), so load-side switching is user-confirmed. Still unconfirmed: SSR active polarity, 3.3V input compatibility, zero-cross behavior, continuous-current/thermal ratings, heat-sink suitability — do not equate LOW with heater OFF.

Heater is 100 W (user-confirmed); cage dimensions 40×40×51 (units not explicitly stated). Cover may be removed during daytime and encloses the cage at night, changing thermal response/heat retention — do not assume PID gains work identically with/without cover. User target 25 °C.

This is an animal/bird enclosure: before enabling PID heating, confirm sensor placement, safe maximum cage temperature, independent thermal cutoff and safe heater placement; do not invent a universal safe cutoff. Closed-loop PID/PWM heater control via DS18B20 is requested while retaining WiFi and OLED monitoring.
