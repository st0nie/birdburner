Target, max power, safety limit and on/off intent are saved. A probe that stops answering is power-cycled first (not a fault unless 3 resets fail). All faults recover automatically: sensor errors once the probe answers and 3 good windows follow, over-temperature once 3 °C below the limit; after a power cut it also waits for 3 good readings. Software cannot replace an independent thermal cutout.
